# Sounds

Named for what they are used for. Synthesised for this project (see the
review pages): `shard_hit_synth`, `shard_break`, `hunter_death`, `swarm_voice`,
`swarm_assemble`, `ground_hum`.

Cut, trimmed and levelled from recordings, all from Pixabay (the downloads
are kept out of the repository, in `samples/used/`, each named for the sound
it became):

- `hunter_growl0`–`hunter_growl2`: capaholiczsfx, "Creature snarl very close" (403154) (`hunter_growls_a`).
- `hunter_growl3`–`hunter_growl6`: freesound_community, "Angry creature" (38085) (`hunter_growls_b`).
- `hunter_pounce`: alesiadavina, "Threatening monster growl, dark creature" (541136).
- `hunter_bite`: freesound_community, "Monster bite" (44538).
- `gun_shot`: freesound_community, "Perdition 1911 pistol" (82365).
- `gun_shot_blast`: freesound_community, "080902_shotgun" (39753), from 0.04 s to 1.35 s, levelled to `gun_shot`'s blast.
- `gun_shot_rolling`: freesound_community, "Shotgun firing 3" (14483), from 0.045 s to 1.6 s (the last 0.25 s faded), levelled to `gun_shot`'s blast.
- `gun_reload`: freesound_community, "Assault rifle manipulation 01" (102921), the first take.
- `player_pain`: freesound_community, "Male hurt 7" (48124).
- `player_step0`–`player_step5`: six steps cut from a download (`player_steps`; its author not recorded).
- `bishop_cast`: rescopicsound, "Elemental magic spell cast D" (228349), from 1.1 s to 4.0 s (its peak 0.6 s in, as the lightning leaves; its tail under the strands).
- `bishop_death`: universfield, "Horror scream" (131025).
- `bishop_call`: yodguard, "Dark magic 2" (378654), all of it (1.6 s; the silence after cut).
- `bishop_blink`: yodguard, "Wind magic 5" (378630).
- `bishop_pain`: a download (`bishop_pain`; its author not recorded), from 1.15 s (past its rising start) to 2.6 s.
- `bishop_mote_burst`: yodguard, "Iced magic 6" (378606), from 0.22 s (just before its peak) to 1.4 s.
- `stalker_step`: yodguard, "Spear thrust 3" (382407), from 0.25 s to 0.75 s (its one hit, its start cut).
- `stalker_shot`: freesound_community, "Space laser shot" (103539), from 0.03 s (its start) to 0.9 s.
- `stalker_impact`: freesound_community, "Explosion" (36210), all of it (1.9 s).
- `stalker_pain`: freesound_community, "Ship creaks 2" (107801), from 0.02 s to 2.45 s (the silence after cut).
- `shard_hit_stab`: ragecore29, "Sword stabbing flesh with splatter 1 (3x)" (588319), its middle stab, from 1.33 s to 1.93 s (`shard_hit`).
