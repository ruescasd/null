# Sounds

Named for what they are used for. Synthesised for this project (see the
review pages): `shard_hit`, `shard_break`, `hunter_death`, `swarm_voice`,
`swarm_assemble`, `ground_hum`.

Cut, trimmed and levelled from recordings, all from Pixabay (the downloads
are kept out of the repository, in `samples/used/`, each named for the sound
it became):

- `hunter_growl0`–`hunter_growl2`: capaholiczsfx, "Creature snarl very close" (403154) (`hunter_growls_a`).
- `hunter_growl3`–`hunter_growl6`: freesound_community, "Angry creature" (38085) (`hunter_growls_b`).
- `hunter_pounce`: alesiadavina, "Threatening monster growl, dark creature" (541136).
- `hunter_bite`: freesound_community, "Monster bite" (44538).
- `gun_shot`: freesound_community, "Perdition 1911 pistol" (82365).
- `gun_reload`: freesound_community, "Assault rifle manipulation 01" (102921), the first take.
- `player_pain`: freesound_community, "Male hurt 7" (48124).
- `player_step0`–`player_step5`: six steps cut from a download (`player_steps`; its author not recorded).
- `bishop_cast`: yodguard, "Casting magic 4" (382380), its swell from 0.9 s to 2.9 s (its peak a second in, as the motes are loosed).
- `bishop_death`: universfield, "Horror scream" (131025).
- `bishop_call`: yodguard, "Dark magic 3" (378655), its first 1.6 s.
- `bishop_blink`: yodguard, "Wind magic 5" (378630).
- `bishop_pain`: a download (`bishop_pain`; its author not recorded), from 1.15 s (past its rising start) to 2.6 s.
